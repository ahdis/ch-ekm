The logical models describe the content of the paper reporting forms independently of FHIR: one element per form item, with its cardinality and, for coded items, the answer list. They are the source the [Questionnaires](questionnaire.html) are built from (each question refers to its element in `Questionnaire.item.definition`), and each model carries a mapping that shows where every form item ends up in the [FHIR document](document.html). The mappings are listed on the *Mappings* tab of each model.

### Logical models per disease

Each disease has a logical model for its whole form. It combines the models of the sections its form contains, in form order. Where a disease's form differs from the shared model of a section (for example initials instead of the full name, or a disease-specific answer list), the disease has its own section model, which is derived from the shared one and only states the differences.

### Shared section models

These models describe the sections that recur across the reporting forms. They are used unchanged by every disease whose form contains the section, unless the disease has its own section model derived from it (last column).
